This idea is what got me to change my views on gender/sexuality. Let me explain:

## Radical Acceptance

The post that introduced the idea to me was someone with OCD who would turn around halfway to work because they were convinced they left the hair dryer on. Their therapist told them to just bring the hair dryer with them so they could see it wasn’t on.

It gets to the idea that the most effective treatment for neurodivergence is often just adapting to the neurodivergence. This reframing is what helps break out of spirals. When I finally sought treatment for my Attention Deficit Disorder, the medication I began did amazing things at helping me be able to focus.

But before the medication could do its work, I had to accept that my brain was different. That the cycles of going from "kick-awesome" to "totally burnt out" weren't a moral failing on my part but a simple fact of my brain chemistry. That what I had read for my whole life as my "laziness" was in fact a brain that didn't start tasks easily.

As I said, medication helps with this. But it's also important for me to find ways to work _with_ my brain instead of against it. This means doing things like taking time to write down important information, tell Siri to set reminders, and break tasks into manageable pieces. While others may scoff—or even be offended!—that I can't "just remember" something, I know that I'll enjoy my life more by allowing myself to do things the "wrong" way.

So what does this have to do with gender and sexuality? Well... it depends.
